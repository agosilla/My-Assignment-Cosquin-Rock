Cosquin Rock 2027

Two days of music, friends and unforgettable experiences in the mountains.

About the Event

Cosquín Rock is one of the most important music festivals in Argentina. It brings together thousands of people and artists for two days of live music, entertainment and shared experiences.

The festival takes place on February 6 and 7, 2027, at the Aeródromo Santa María de Punilla, in the Punilla Valley, Córdoba. Surrounded by the mountains, the landscape becomes an important part of the festival experience.

The festival runs for two days, from 2:00 PM until 2:00 AM. Each day offers a different combination of artists, stages and experiences, making every visit unique. During the day, visitors can move between different stages, listen to their favorite artists, discover new music and share the experience with friends and other music lovers. There are also activities for all ages and food stands.

What to Expect

Live Music

Experience different artists and musical styles across several stages. From major Argentine bands to emerging artists, there is always something new to discover during the festival.

Festival Atmosphere

Thousands of people come together to sing, dance and enjoy the music. The crowd, the stages and the landscape create a unique festival atmosphere.

The Stages

Cosquín Rock has several stages, each with its own atmosphere.

  1. The Norte stage: One of the main stages of the festival, located at one end of the venue. It hosts some of the biggest performances of the weekend.
  2. The Sur stage: Another main stage located at the opposite end of the festival. Moving between Norte and Sur is part of the experience.
  3. The Montaña stage: Located between the Norte and Sur stages, the Montaña stage offers an experience with the mountains as a backdrop.
  4. The Boomerang stage: A space dedicated to discovering different sounds and alternative / emerging artists.
  5. The Casita del Blues stage: A more intimate stage dedicated to blues.
  6. The Plaza Electronic stage: A space dedicated to electronic music, offering a different sound and atmosphere within the festival.
  7. The Sorpresa stage: An unexpected stage that adds an element of surprise to the festival experience. You never know what you might find.

Important! The Norte and Sur stages are approximately 900 meters apart, with the Montaña stage located between them. Plan enough time to walk between stages during the festival.

Pocket Cash

Your wristband is your wallet inside Cosquín Rock. Pocket Cash is the festival's payment system. All purchases inside the festival must be paid for using the money loaded onto your wristband, so you do not need to carry cash or cards around the venue.

How to Load Money

Before the festival

You can load money onto your Pocket Cash wristband through the official festival website before arriving. Loading your balance in advance is recommended so you can start enjoying the festival without having to stop at a charging point.

During the festival

If you need to add more money, there are authorized charging points inside the festival. You can load your Pocket Cash using a credit or debit card, digital wallets or cash.

No cash, no card

Once you are inside the festival, your wristband is all you need to pay for food, drinks and other purchases. Remember to keep it with you throughout the event.

What happens to the money left over?

Don't worry if you do not spend everything. Any remaining balance can be returned after the festival following the official refund process.

Our Tip

Load your Pocket Cash before arriving. It will save you time and let you focus on what really matters: the music.

Important Information

What to Bring

  • ID
  • Wristband
  • Comfortable clothes and shoes
  • Sunscreen
  • A hat
  • If you bought your festival cup on the first day, don't forget to bring it the next day!
  • Everything you need to spend a long day. Dont worry if you forget something, you can buy there.

It is important to be prepared for different weather conditions because the festival takes place outdoors.

Recommendations

Arrive early, check the festival schedule and plan which artists you want to see. Stay hydrated, take breaks when necessary and wear comfortable shoes because you will probably spend a lot of time walking around the festival.
